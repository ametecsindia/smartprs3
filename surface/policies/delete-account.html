This page explains how to delete a SmartPRS account and its data — from the SmartPRS mobile app or the website. SmartPRS is provided by Ametecs India Private Limited.

1. Employee logins (your company uses SmartPRS)

Your login is created and owned by your employer. To delete it, ask your company's SmartPRS administrator (HR) to deactivate or remove your login under Administration → User Management, and to remove your phone under Administration → Mobile Devices. You can also write to us and we will pass the request to your employer.

  • Deleted: your login, your registered phone and its sign-in tokens.
  • Kept by your employer: attendance, leave, payroll and statutory records your employer must keep by law (for example under the labour codes and income-tax rules), for the period the law requires.

2. Explore Demo accounts

A demo company made with Explore Demo — and every login and sample record in it — is deleted automatically when the demo ends (7 days unless extended). To delete it sooner, email us from the address you used for the demo, or use the request below.

3. Company (client) accounts

The company's administrator can ask us to close the account. Workspace data is kept for 90 days after the subscription ends so it can be exported, then permanently deleted, as described in our Privacy Policy.

4. How to ask

Email support@ametecsindia.com with the subject Delete my SmartPRS account, from the email address of the login, and tell us the company address (for example smartprs.com/c/yourcompany). We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.
